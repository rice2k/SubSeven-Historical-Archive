# Historical files found locally

This page records SubSeven/Sub7 material recovered from a **local historical preservation collection** and explains how each class of artifact contributes to the archive. The collection mixes original-era package records, old website crawls, screenshots, later preservation copies, secondary research exports and modern fan material, so provenance is recorded separately from historical claims.

## 7. Duplicate policy

Duplicates are **not discarded from the historical record**. Instead:

- one copy is marked **canonical**;
- additional same-name/same-size records are marked **duplicate**;
- alternate filenames or uncertain byte identity are marked **alternate/variant**;
- every duplicate remains visible in [`../data/historical-file-inventory.csv`](../data/historical-file-inventory.csv).

This preserves provenance without making the repository itself unnecessarily repetitive.

## 8. Public archive treatment

The repository publishes screenshots, artwork, non-operational HTML, documentation, research tables and historical URL indexes. Classic executable packages and potentially weaponized source packages are documented with filenames, sizes, lineage and source references rather than redistributed as runnable payloads.
