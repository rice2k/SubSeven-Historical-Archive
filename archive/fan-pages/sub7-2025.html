SUB7 LEGACY

Welcome to Mobman's Domain

[ Established 1999 - The Trojan Lives On ]

Enter the world of Sub7, the infamous Remote Access Tool crafted by Mobman. First unleashed on February 28, 1999, Sub7 redefined remote control, giving power to those who dared to wield it. From opening CD trays to capturing webcams, this was the ultimate playground for the underground.

The Legend

Born in Craiova, Romania, Mobman dropped Sub7 v1.0 as a nod to Back Orifice, but it became so much more. Evolving through versions like 2.1.5 "Legends" and the ill-fated 2.3, Sub7 inspired a generation of coders, pranksters, and security pros. Development peaked in 2003, but the echo lingers.
